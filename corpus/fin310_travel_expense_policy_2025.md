# Harbor Point Bank Travel and Expense Policy (2025)

Policy ID: FIN-310
Version: 2025
Effective date: 1 March 2025
Owner: Finance
This version supersedes the 2024 version of FIN-310.

## 1. Scope

This policy governs reimbursement of reasonable costs that employees incur while traveling on bank business, including meals, lodging, airfare and ground transportation.

## 2. Meal Allowance

Travelers receive a flat meal allowance of $75 per day for domestic trips. The allowance is reduced by 25 percent on the first and last day of travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 3. Lodging

Nightly lodging is reimbursed up to $300 in New York City, San Francisco and Boston, and up to $225 in all other locations. Bookings must be made through the corporate travel portal.

## 4. Airfare

Economy class is required for all flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer without additional approval. Business class requires approval from an executive vice president.

## 5. Ground Transportation

Rideshare and taxi costs are reimbursable for business trips. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS mileage rate.

## 6. Expense Submission

Expense reports must be submitted in Concur within 15 days of the end of the trip, with receipts attached for every item above $25.
