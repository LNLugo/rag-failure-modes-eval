# Harbor Point Bank Compensation FAQ for Employees

Policy ID: HR-300
Effective date: 1 January 2025
Owner: Human Resources

## How are annual bonuses decided?

Annual bonuses for employees are discretionary. They are based on individual performance ratings and the bank's overall results, and are paid in March.

## When are salary reviews held?

Salary reviews are held once a year in February, with any increase effective 1 April.

## Who do I contact about my pay?

Contact the HR Service Center through the Workday help tab.
