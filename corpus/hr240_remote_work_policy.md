# Harbor Point Bank Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-240
Effective date: 1 June 2025
Owner: Human Resources

## 1. Eligibility

Roles in technology, finance and corporate functions are eligible for remote work. Branch and customer-facing roles are not eligible.

## 2. Remote Work Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Tuesdays and Wednesdays are designated in-office anchor days for all teams.

## 3. Equipment and Security

Employees working remotely must use a bank-issued laptop and connect through the corporate VPN. Printing customer documents at home is prohibited.
