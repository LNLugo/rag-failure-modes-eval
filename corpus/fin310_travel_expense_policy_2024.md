# Harbor Point Bank Travel and Expense Policy (2024)

Policy ID: FIN-310
Version: 2024
Effective date: 1 February 2024
Owner: Finance

## 1. Scope

This policy covers business travel and expenses for all Harbor Point Bank employees.

## 2. Meals

The daily meal per diem for business travel is $60. The meal per diem for business travel covers breakfast, lunch and dinner and does not require itemized receipts.

## 3. Lodging

The hotel cap for business travel is $250 per night in all cities. Employees must book through the corporate travel portal.

## 4. Airfare

Economy class is required for all flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved by a vice president for flights of 6 hours or longer.

## 5. Expense Submission

Expense reports are due within 30 days of the end of the trip.
