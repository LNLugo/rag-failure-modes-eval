# Harbor Point Bank Code of Conduct

Policy ID: CMP-100
Effective date: 1 January 2025
Owner: Compliance

## 1. Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may not accept gifts from customers or vendors with a value above $100 per year from any single source. Cash gifts of any amount are prohibited.

## 2. Conflicts of Interest

Outside business activities must be disclosed and approved by Compliance before they begin.

## 3. Speaking Up

Employees can report concerns through the Ethics Hotline, which is available 24 hours a day and accepts anonymous reports.
