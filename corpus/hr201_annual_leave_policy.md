# Harbor Point Bank Annual Leave and Time Off Policy

Policy ID: HR-201
Effective date: 1 January 2025
Owner: Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how employees of Harbor Point Bank earn, request and use annual leave and other paid time off. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees in every branch and operations center. Contractors and temporary agency staff are covered by the terms of their own agreements and are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees accrue annual leave each pay period based on continuous service with the bank:

- Less than 2 years of service: 18 days per year
- 2 to 5 years of service: 22 days per year
- More than 5 years of service: 26 days per year

Part-time employees accrue annual leave in proportion to their scheduled weekly hours. Accrual begins on the first day of employment, and leave may be used as soon as it has accrued, subject to manager approval.

## 3. Carryover of Unused Annual Leave

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days are added to the employee's balance on 1 January and must be used by 31 March of that year. Any carried-over days that remain unused after 31 March are forfeited and are not paid out. Managers are expected to plan coverage so that employees have a reasonable opportunity to use their carried-over days during the first quarter, and Human Resources sends a reminder to every affected employee in the second week of February. Requests to extend the 31 March deadline are considered only for documented business needs and must be approved by the department head and Human Resources in writing.

Exception: employees who are within their first year of service on 31 December may not carry over any unused annual leave days. Their remaining balance is paid out in the first payroll of January instead.

## 4. Requesting Leave

Leave requests are submitted through the Workday self-service portal at least 10 business days in advance for absences of three days or more. Shorter absences require 2 business days of notice where possible. Managers respond within 3 business days.

## 5. Sick Leave

All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on separation. Absences of more than 3 consecutive days require a note from a medical provider.

## 6. Parental Leave

Employees with at least 12 months of service are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the event.
