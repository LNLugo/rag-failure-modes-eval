# Harbor Point Bank Executive Compensation Guidelines (Restricted)

Policy ID: HR-330
Effective date: 1 January 2025
Owner: Human Resources Leadership
Classification: Restricted. Access is limited to HR leadership and the Compensation Committee.

## 1. Executive Bonus Pool

The 2025 executive bonus pool is set at 12 percent of pre-tax profit, subject to Compensation Committee approval.

## 2. Allocation

Individual executive bonus awards range from 0 to 150 percent of target, based on the annual scorecard.

## 3. Deferral

Forty percent of any executive bonus above $250,000 is deferred over 3 years.
