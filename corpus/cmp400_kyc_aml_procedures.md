# Harbor Point Bank KYC and AML Operating Procedures

Policy ID: CMP-400
Effective date: 1 April 2025
Owner: Compliance

## 1. Overview

These procedures describe the customer due diligence forms and escalation steps used by branch and operations staff. Each form or procedure below has its own section. Staff must complete the form named for the situation and attach it to the customer record in the onboarding system.

## 2. Form KYC-3A

Purpose: Form KYC-3A is the individual identity verification form.
When to use: complete it for every new individual customer before the account is opened.
What it records: legal name, date of birth, residential address and government identification.
Who signs: the customer and the opening officer.

## 3. Form KYC-3B

Purpose: Form KYC-3B is the identity refresh form.
When to use: complete it for an existing individual customer when their identification expires or their contact details change.
What it records: updated identification and contact details.
Who signs: the customer and the servicing officer.

## 4. Form KYC-7A

Purpose: Form KYC-7A is the legal entity profile.
When to use: complete it for every new legal entity customer before the account is opened.
What it records: legal name, formation documents, registered address and nature of business.
Who signs: an authorized representative of the entity and the opening officer.

## 5. Form KYC-7B

Purpose: Form KYC-7B is the beneficial ownership certification.
When to use: complete it for every new legal entity customer before the account is opened.
What it records: every individual who owns 25 percent or more of the entity, plus one individual with significant control.
Who signs: the individual opening the account on behalf of the entity, and the opening officer.

## 6. Form KYC-7C

Purpose: Form KYC-7C is the trust and foundation supplement.
When to use: complete it for every new trust or foundation customer before the account is opened.
What it records: trustees, settlors, protectors and beneficiaries.
Who signs: a trustee and the opening officer.

## 7. Procedure AML-114

Purpose: Procedure AML-114 is the unusual activity escalation.
When to use: staff who observe unusual customer activity.
What it requires: escalate to the BSA Officer within 2 business days using the AML-114 referral in the case management system.
Who acts: the observing employee and their supervisor.

## 8. Procedure AML-115

Purpose: Procedure AML-115 is the sanctions screening escalation.
When to use: staff who receive a potential sanctions match from the screening system.
What it requires: escalate to the Sanctions Team within 4 hours and hold the transaction until it is cleared.
Who acts: the operations analyst and the Sanctions Team.

## 9. Procedure AML-120

Purpose: Procedure AML-120 is cash transaction reporting.
When to use: cash transactions totaling more than $10,000 for one customer in a single business day.
What it requires: file a Currency Transaction Report within 15 days.
Who acts: the branch operations lead.

## 10. Record Retention

All KYC and AML records are retained for 5 years after the account is closed.
